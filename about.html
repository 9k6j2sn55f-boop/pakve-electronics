Our 25-Year Journey & Legacy

From an electronic repairing workshop in 2001 to a multi-brand showroom in Kalamb, Maharashtra.

Background Story

Built on Technical Precision & Hard Work

Our shop was started in 2001, beginning with repairing services. As we continued repairing electronic appliances, we developed deep technical expertise in circuit durability and appliance mechanics, which gradually led us into the sales business.

In 2015, we opened a retail shop selling Smart TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners. Thanks to the tremendous response and trust of our customers, we successfully operated that shop for 9 years.

In 2023, we took a major step forward and opened our large-scale showroom on Dhoki Road, Kalamb for electronics and furniture. Today, our showroom offers a wide range of electronic products alongside handcrafted beds, wardrobes, dressing tables, office chairs, and dining sets.

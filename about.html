Our 25-Year Journey & Heritage

From a dedicated electronic repairing workshop in 2001 to a premier multi-brand showroom on Dhoki Road, Kalamb.

Background Story

Built on Technical Knowledge & Community Trust

Our journey began in 2001 as an electronic repairing service shop. By diagnosing and repairing thousands of household appliances down to their circuit boards and motors, we developed a first-hand understanding of which brands deliver genuine durability.

That deep engineering foundation allowed us to expand into retail sales in 2015, operate successfully for 9 years, and culminate in our modern Electronics & Solid Wood Furniture Showroom in 2023.

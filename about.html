Our Journey

The Milestones That Built Our Trust

Over two decades of dedication to technical expertise, genuine products, and local customer service.

Milestone 01 • 2001

The Repairing Services Foundation

Started as a dedicated electronics and home appliance repair hub in Kalamb. Built deep foundational technical expertise, mastering circuit repairs, cooling systems, and genuine hardware servicing.

Milestone 02 • 2015

First Retail Sales Shop

Expanded from technical servicing into direct retail, opening the first consumer electronics sales outlet. Introduced Kalamb families to authentic branded appliances with verified manufacturer warranties.

Milestone 03 • Present

Grand Showroom Launch on Dhoki Rd

Inaugurated our flagship multi-story showroom on Dhoki Road, uniting top-tier consumer electronics with handcrafted solid teak wood furniture, 0% EMI financing, and doorstep delivery.
